Lumi v4 · extends v2.12 · read-only views

Her files, pictures, journal and thoughts

Everything from v2.12 stays as it was. v4 adds seven views of what she keeps on the server, built from the existing parts. The new interface copy is English (as the brief asks); her content stays Ukrainian. Sections 08–18 below, with 08b for the right panels; every window is live.

1Views replace the chat columnA row of tabs over the content column — Chat · Files · Gallery · Journal · Thoughts · State · Schedule. The portrait panel and the status line never move; the row takes its 42px from the content column.
2The chat keeps listeningWhile another view is open she can still write. The Chat tab then carries the 6px accent square the thoughts strip already uses. ⌘1…⌘7 switch views.
3Reading, not browsingHer files, the journal and the mood are set at 17/1.7 on a 68ch measure, about 70 Cyrillic characters a line. Sizes, dates and paths stay 11–12px and faint.
4Nothing new in the languageKickers, meta grids, hairline rows, the session divider, the closeness meter, the pulse, the command-block surface. The only new pieces are the tabs, the outline and the biorhythm curve.
5Her text is never HTMLMarkdown is parsed into built elements. Her own links open in the reader; external links show their address with ↗ and are never opened in the window.
6Room for actionsFiles and the picture view keep an empty action area at the right of their header and meta column for upload, “show this to her” and download in a later version.
Assumptions to confirm· No pictures were attached. Every picture uses one of eight slots (lili-pic-1 … lili-pic-8): drop her real pictures on the first eight tiles of 11.1 and they fill the whole gallery, the reader and the chat picture.· The v2.12 chrome in these mockups still reads in Ukrainian (it was drawn that way); only the new strings are English. strings.en.add.json holds them.· “Now” is Monday 16 November 2026, 23:58. Folder contents, journal days and thoughts are invented to the brief’s counts; the day cap’s unit is not stated, so it shows as a bare number.
08

The view row

Try it
Click a tab or press ⌘1…⌘7 (click inside the window first).
Files: open a folder, a .md file, a picture. Back / forward with ⌘[ and ⌘].
In the reader, click an accent link to open another of her files; the dotted one is external and only shows its address.
Gallery: scroll to load more; Enter or click opens the full view, ← → step, Esc closes.
Journal: ← → move between days.
Thoughts: open Kind, pick several; switch Spoken / Silent.
08b

Right panels — pin views beside the chat

Any view except Chat can be pinned to the right, to the bottom right, or both, with the Pin buttons at the end of the view row. The right column then holds one or two panels, each with its own tabs. The chat stays in the middle, so you can read her file or her journal while you talk. Drag the left edge to set the width (280–520), the rule between the panels to set the split; ⌘\ folds the column into a rail. The views in the panels use their narrow layout. On a phone there are no side panels; pinned views stay as tabs.

Try it
Pin any open view with the two buttons at the right of the tab row: right, or bottom right. Pressed means pinned there; press again to unpin.
Each panel has its own tabs; × unpins the one shown.
Drag the left edge or the rule between the panels; with the keyboard, focus it and use the arrows.
⌘\ or the » button folds the column into a rail.
09

Files

A path bar where every folder is a button, then a quiet table: folders first, name with a folder or file icon, size, created, modified. Picture rows open the full view; text rows open the reader; other types are listed, muted, and not shown.

10

The markdown reader

The most-read view. A 68ch column at 17/1.7, headings h1–h4, lists, tables, quotes, code, a rule and her pictures. An outline sits beside long pages (four or more headings) and follows the scroll; on a phone it folds under Contents.

Try it
Formatted / Raw switches the same file to its source with line numbers.
Ідеї на зиму and нотатки про дощ are her own files: they open here, and Back returns.
Вікіпедія is external: dotted, with its address, never opened.
The picture opens the full view.
11

Gallery and the full picture

Square crops in an auto-fill grid with month dividers, newest first, 100 a page (here 40, then more as you scroll). A folder filter on the right. The full view shows the picture as large as fits in its natural proportions, with its path, date, size and dimensions, and Open in Files.

Try it
Filter by folder: All · art · art/sketches · pictures · notes.
Scroll to the end to load the next page.
Open a picture, then ← → and Esc. Open in Files jumps to its folder.
12

Journal

Days grouped by month on the left; the day reads on the right. The stamp — Mood, Biorhythms, Forecast — is data, so it sits on the surface fill with kicker labels. Her entries stay on the ground at reading size, each with its time in a gutter.

Try it
Pick a day in the list or press ← →.
Before 1 August the days have no stamp.
13

Thoughts

The full stream behind the strip under her portrait (which now links here). Time in a gutter, the kind as a kicker, the text in full colour, her caption for the emotion, and a mark on spoken thoughts. Day dividers; Kind (several at once), Spoken / Silent / All and a date jump; more loads at the end.

Try it
Kind opens the 17 directive kinds as toggles.
Spoken filters to the 6% she said aloud.
Scroll to load earlier days.
14

State of the day

The mood reading in full at reading size, its short resolution set large, and the theme. Beside it: the three biorhythms with their place on the cycle, closeness on the v2.12 meter, and a held place for the day’s plan.

15

Schedule

Quiet hours, the day cap and what fires next, above a table of directives: name, kind, the rule in words, the last firing and the next due. The next one carries the surface fill, the 2px accent inset and a Next label.
