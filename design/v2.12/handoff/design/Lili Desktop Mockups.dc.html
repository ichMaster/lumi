Лілі — chat and portrait in one window

Every state from the brief, on the Modernist system turned dark. UI copy is Ukrainian; notes are English. Section 01 is live — type into it.

1Modernist, invertedThe system’s ink #201e1d becomes the ground and its paper #f3f2f2 the type. Zero radius, 2px rules, everything flush left. The light theme is the system as shipped.
2One accent, chosen by the dayEach theme swaps a single --color-accent (table in 07). The system red stays as furious and as the fallback. The portraits carry the colour; the chrome stays neutral.
3The portrait comes firstThe left column is clamp(300px, 36%, 440px) with a square portrait. Two stacked layers crossfade in 480 ms, so the layout never jumps. The caption is set in display weight, with theme and closeness under it and the mood folded to two lines.
4Messages on a gutter, no bubblesAn 84px label column holds who · time · source. My lines sit on a surface fill and hug the text; hers sit on the ground at full measure (about 70 characters). The narrow layout folds the gutter into a label row.
5Thoughts stay out of the chatBy default they sit in a strip under her portrait: her inner life lives beside her face, never in the chat. Two alternatives are in 1a and 1c.
6Technical details start foldedA 30px status line. A click opens model, latency, tokens, turns, the think-block mode (shown / collapsed / hidden) and the chat text size.
Assumptions to confirm · No portraits were attached. Each slot is named after its file (theme/emotion/NN.png) — drop a PNG on any slot; every window with the same id picks it up on reload. · Archivo’s web build has no Cyrillic, so Ukrainian text renders in SF Pro (the system font), which the brief requires anyway. Latin labels stay in Archivo. · The host lili.home:8443, the model lili-main, the path ~/.config/lumi/client.toml and the lumi token command are placeholders.

1

The thought stream — three options

Her quiet thoughts never enter the chat. The default is 1b. Each option keeps the same item: a kind, a time, the text, and her caption for the emotion. A small accent square marks unread thoughts.

1aSide drawer. A 46px rail with a counter opens a 300px column; the chat narrows but keeps its measure.
1bStrip under the portrait (default). Shows the latest thought; «Усі» expands the list in place.
1cOn her face. A small counter on the portrait; opened, the thoughts lie over the portrait like a veil. Zero chat footprint.

Try next: “go with 1a but open it from the status line” · “in 1c, show one thought on the portrait without opening” · “a stream that fades thoughts in over time”
