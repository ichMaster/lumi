# Results of the system prompt optimization

A comparison of two dumps of the system prompt:

| | File | Size |
|---|---|---|
| **Before** | `.lumi/prompt-2026-06-20-2236.md` | 60,278 characters · 9,217 words |
| **After** | `.lumi/prompt-2026-06-22-2136.md` | 25,377 characters · 4,021 words |

> Characters are counted as code points (in UTF-8 bytes it is roughly twice as much: 105 KB → 44 KB).

---

## 1. In short

- The system prompt became **≈ 2.4 times more compact** (−58% characters, −56% words), and that is on
  **every turn**.
- The main point is that it is **not just "less" but "smarter"**: we removed "dumping the whole memory every
  turn" and added "pulling in exactly what is needed". So the total volume fell, **while relevance rose**.
- The saving comes almost entirely from **memory** (−84%); the **canon/persona itself even grew a little**
  (+19%), but on purpose (the rewritten Sthira persona).

---

## 2. Where the saving comes from (by group)

| Group | Before | After | Δ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Canon / persona** | 10,122 | 12,014 | **+19%** |
| **Memory** (weeks / days / conversations / facts) | 39,997 | 6,243 | **−84%** |
| **Dynamics** (RAG / mood / thoughts / style) | 10,159 | 7,120 | **−30%** |
| **Total** | **60,278** | **25,377** | **−58%** |

**The canon grew not by oversight but on purpose**: the persona was rewritten (Лілі → **Sthira** (Стхіра),
«чітіна, та-що-лишається», "a chitina, the one who stays"), the `## Творче «я»` ("The creative self") and
`## Як тече розмова` ("How the conversation flows") blocks were added, and the prohibitions were
restructured. This is a deepening of the character, not a regression. All the real optimization is in the
**memory** block.

---

## 3. Memory in detail

| Section | Before | After | Δ | What produced the saving |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `## Останні тижні` (recent weeks) | 5,339 | 713 | **−87%** | the weekly digests compressed into **one-line gists** (v0.34, `LUMI_MEMORY_INDEX`) |
| `## Останні дні` (recent days) | 9,787 | 872 | **−91%** | the daily digests turned into gists (v0.34) |
| `## Останні розмови (детально)` (recent conversations, in detail) | 16,268 | 1,935 | **−88%** | the conversation format switched to **gist** (v0.35, `LUMI_SESSION_FORMAT=gist`) |
| `## Факти` (facts) | 8,603 | 2,723 | **−68%** | instead of the 73-line digest, **44 core facts** (core-only, v0.36) + a cleanup of **251 obsolete** ones |

So the four "heavy" memory layers that used to take **~40 thousand characters every turn** now take
**~6 thousand**.

---

## 4. What grew ON PURPOSE (and rightly so)

| Section | Before | After | Δ | Why this is good |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `# Релевантні моменти минулого` (relevant moments of the past; message auto-RAG) | 1,929 | 3,518 | **+82%** | more **relevant** context from past conversations, fitted to this very turn's request |
| `# Релевантні факти` (relevant facts; auto fact-RAG, **new** in v0.36) | 0 | 267 | **new** | an automatic push of the top-K relevant facts for the current message |

That is the essence of the shift: **the volume went down, while the amount of *pertinent* context went up.**

---

## 5. The architectural shift (the main idea)

**Before:** inject the **whole** memory every turn: 1,229 facts (through the digest) and the full summaries of
weeks, days and conversations. The prompt grew linearly with the history of the conversation.

**Now** there are three channels to the facts instead of one "dump":

1. **The static core (push, always)**: 44 `core` facts, covering identity, family, work, values, and
   **boundaries and agreements**. The `## Факти` block.
2. **The relevant (push, per request)**: the top-K facts (`# Релевантні факти`) and the top-K moments
   (`# Релевантні моменти минулого`), picked for the current message.
3. **On demand (pull)**: the `recall(scope=facts|messages|all)` tool, for when Sthira needs to dig something
   up herself.

This is the canonical **"cache the static, RAG the dynamic"** pattern from
[PROMPT_OPTIMIZATION.md](PROMPT_OPTIMIZATION.md): the stable prefix (canon + core) is cached, and what changes
is pulled in every turn.

---

## 6. Quality, not only size

- **251 noise facts were removed** (`obsolete`): hallucinated names (Володимир / Igor / Олена / Орен),
  meta-lines («Нічого вартого запамʼятовування…», "Nothing worth remembering…"; «Порожньо», "Empty"), and
  duplicates. Recall became **cleaner**: there is less chance that junk surfaces.
- **The 44 core facts were selected manually (with Opus)** rather than generated by a weaker model's digest,
  which gives a more accurate "identity core"; the boundaries and agreements are **pinned** (the cap will never
  drop them).
- The noise facts stay in the store (they are not deleted), so the change is reversible and kept for audit.

---

## 7. Cost saving estimate (Opus 4.8)

The model is `claude-opus-4-8` (the premium tier). Anthropic prices (per 1M tokens):
**input $5 · output $25 · cache-read $0.50 · cache-write $10**. The volume is about **1,600 turns and $244 for
June**; caching already saves **~$232** (without it, the bill would have been ~$476).

The system prompt is paid for **every turn**, mostly as **cache-read** (a warm cache) or as
**cache-write + input** (at session start). So the −58% in prompt size hits the cost of every turn directly.

### Real data from `usage-ledger.jsonl` (before → after)

| Metric per turn | 20 Jun (before) | 22 Jun (after) | Δ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Cost / turn** | **$0.158** | **$0.130** | **−17%** |
| cache-read tokens / turn | 43,233 | 29,571 | **−32%** |
| cache-write tokens / turn | 2,478 | 1,706 | **−31%** |
| input tokens / turn | 17,863 | 14,879 | **−17%** |

The **~32% drop in cache-read/write** is a direct sign that the cached prefix (canon + memory) got smaller: it
is cheaper both to set up (cache-write at session start) and to reread (cache-read every turn).

### Structural estimate

The system prompt: **~27,400 → ~11,500 tokens** (−58%, estimated from characters), that is, **~16 thousand
fewer tokens on every turn** in the part that is always paid for. On a warm cache (cache-read $0.50/1M) this is
≈ **$0.008/turn**; on a cold start (cache-write $10/1M) it is ≈ **$0.16 per session**.

### Projection

At ~1,600 turns/month the saving is **~$0.027/turn ≈ $40–45/month (~$480–540/year)**, that is, **about −17%**
of the current ~$244/month.

### The main, long-term gain

The biggest value is not a one-off: before, the prompt **grew linearly with the history** (1,229 facts through
the digest plus ever-longer summaries), so the cost of a turn **crept up week by week**. Now the core is
**fixed** (44 facts), and the rest is pulled in as top-K through RAG, so **the cost of a turn is stabilized**
rather than growing. Over a horizon of months this saves far more than the one-off −17%.

### Caveats

The estimates are **approximate**: the cost of a turn depends on the session length (cache-read accumulates
within a session), the length of the history and the size of the reply; the optimization was rolled out
gradually (v0.34→v0.36), and the persona (Sthira) changed in parallel. The daily spread is $13–37. So go by the
**order of magnitude** (~−17% per turn, −32% cache tokens), not by an exact figure to the cent. The source of
truth is the Anthropic bill, not these estimates.

---

## 8. Summary

- A **~2.4× more compact** system prompt on every turn (−58%).
- **Memory −84%**, while there is **more relevant context** (the moments auto-RAG +82%, the new fact-RAG).
- Fewer tokens per turn make it **cheaper and faster**, with no loss in memory accuracy, and rather a **gain**
  (the core is verified, the noise removed).

*Caveat: the reduction from 60k to 25k also includes the rewritten persona (Sthira), which made the canon
slightly bigger. The "pure" effect of the memory optimization is the **Memory −84%** line (≈ 40k → 6k
characters).*
