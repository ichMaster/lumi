## Findings

### 1. 🟠 MEDIUM — a hung ElevenLabs call holds the outbox, and a shutdown drops the queue

**Where:** `LineVoicer._voice` and `LineVoicer.close` in [server/voice.py](../../../server/voice.py);
`voice_of` in [server/__main__.py](../../../server/__main__.py); `ElevenLabsTTS` in
[voice/tts.py](../../../voice/tts.py).

**Failure scenario:**
- `LineVoicer` is the server's one ordered outbox writer, so while a line is being voiced every later write waits
  behind it: her next lines, your mirrored lines, a photo she chose to send.
- `ElevenLabsTTS` builds the SDK client with its default timeout, 240 s. A call that hangs (the API slow, a network
  stall) keeps Telegram silent for up to 4 minutes per line. The window shows «voicing…» the whole time.
- At shutdown (a deploy restarts the container) `close()` drains for 30 s and then marks the writer closed. Whatever
  is still queued stays in the worker thread, which is a daemon thread and dies with the process. Those lines never
  reach the outbox, and nothing is logged. The line in flight is lost the same way.

**Proposed fix:**
- the server's TTS gets a bounded timeout (60 s): `ElevenLabsTTS(..., timeout=)`, passed by `voice_of` only (the
  local voicer keeps the SDK's default);
- `close()`, when its drain runs out, writes everything still queued in the caller, unvoiced (`voice: null`), and
  the line in flight too; the worker drops its late append for a line already written; one log line names how many
  were written unvoiced.

### 2. 🟠 MEDIUM — a long reply reaches Telegram cut at 1024 characters

**Where:** `forward` in [telegram/outbound.py](../../../telegram/outbound.py) (the `stored` and `old` sends);
`caption_for` in `run()`.

**Failure scenario:**
- A voice message carries her words as its caption, and Telegram caps a caption at 1024 characters. `caption_for`
  cuts the rendered text there.
- A reply of 2500 characters (a news summary, an explanation) goes as a voice of the whole text and a caption of the
  first 1024. In Telegram the remaining 1476 characters can only be heard, never read.
- Past 3000 characters the server voices only the first 3000 (`VOICE_MAX_CHARS`), so the end of the reply reaches
  Telegram neither as text nor as voice.
- The v0.13 voice mode cut the caption the same way. v4.6 rewrote this send and kept it; prod runs with the voice on.

**Proposed fix:** when her words don't fit a caption, send the voice without a caption, then the words as text
(chunked by the text path), as `send_photo_record` already does for a long caption. `forward`'s `caption_for` returns
the full text, and `forward` decides.

### 3. 🟡 LOW — «voicing…» never resolves when the `voice` push is lost

**Where:** `voiceLine` / `voiceReady` in [desktop/ui/app.js](../../../desktop/ui/app.js).

**Failure scenario:** the server restarts while a line is being voiced. The window reconnects to the new boot, whose
replay buffer is empty, so the `voice` push for that line never comes. The line keeps «voicing…» until the window
reloads. The same happens if a listener's queue overflows and drops the push.

**Proposed fix:** after the state of a fresh connection arrives, or after a timeout, ask the server for the lines
still marked «voicing…» (`GET /v1/voice/<id>` answers 404 or the audio) and settle each one. Backlog: rare, harmless
(the text is there), and it needs a small client design.

### 4. 🟡 LOW — lines from the history have no ▶

**Where:** the history load in [desktop/ui/app.js](../../../desktop/ui/app.js); the `Message` record.

**Failure scenario:** the owner reopens the window. Lines that arrive by replay carry their voice id; lines loaded
from the session history don't, because the voice id isn't kept on the message. Their files still live for 30 days,
but there is no ▶ to play them.

**Proposed fix:** keep the voice id on her `Message` (an additive field, like `move`) and send it with the history.
Backlog: a memory-record change, outside the phase's scope; the spec promises ▶ on lines as they come.

### 5. 🟡 LOW — `voice` pushes take replay slots

**Where:** `_REPLAYABLE` in [server/app.py](../../../server/app.py); `her_thought`.

**Failure scenario:**
- Each voiced line adds a `voice` event to the 50-event replay buffer, so a reconnecting window now replays about
  half as many lines as before.
- A shown thought (`sched_show_to_outbox`) is voiced for Telegram. Its `voice` push names an id that no `message`
  carried, which every window ignores.

**Proposed fix:** fold the readiness into the replayed `message` item instead of keeping a separate event, and skip
the push for a thought. Cleanup: nothing breaks, the window just sees less history.

### 6. 🟡 LOW — the daemon's voice mode follows `telegram.voice`, not the switch

**Where:** `delivery` / `run()` in [telegram/outbound.py](../../../telegram/outbound.py).

**Failure scenario:**
- With `telegram.voice: true` and the switch turned off in the window, her lines reach Telegram as text, but your
  mirrored lines are still skipped (the daemon's voice mode).
- With `telegram.voice: false` and the switch turned on, her lines arrive as voice messages and your lines as
  «💻 …» between them.
- This is the deviation recorded in the execution report: in voice mode your lines stay out of Telegram, as before
  v4.6.

**Proposed fix:** the daemon reads `<data root>/voice.json` for the mode, or the owner decides that your lines always
or never go. Backlog: the owner's call.

## What's solid

- **One writer, one order:** every server outbox write goes through `LineVoicer`, so a photo or your next line can
  never overtake a line being voiced, and only one thread ever appends.
- **The id before the line:** `new_id()` is taken before the `message` push, so a window sees the id first and the
  `voice` push always after it; a push that arrives first is remembered (`voice.ready`).
- **Degrades to text everywhere:** a TTS failure, an empty answer, a store error, a missing or unconvertible file,
  a malformed name on a record — each ends in the words, never in a failed turn or a wedged daemon.
- **No new TTS call in Telegram for the server's lines:** a record that names a stored file is sent from that file;
  `voice: null` is text; only a record without the key (pre-v4.6, in-process) goes the old way.
- **Bounded and contained:** the files live outside her sandbox, under the user's folder, 32-hex names only, written
  through a temp file and a rename; the expiry removes at most 500 per pass, one folder level, never a link; the
  route reads no link and answers 404 for anything else; the room's token is refused.
- **Nothing secret or private in the log:** the id, the length, the time; the exception's type and first 200
  characters.
- **Off by default where it must be:** a server without a voice setup has no `voice` on the state and 404 on both
  routes; the switch off → no TTS call, no `voice` on the outcome, the outcome byte-identical.

## Fixes applied

### #1 — a bounded TTS call, and a stop that writes what is left (a9e4137)

- **Change:**
  - `ElevenLabsTTS` takes an optional `timeout` (seconds per call; `None` keeps the SDK's 240 s). The server passes
    `TTS_TIMEOUT_S` = 60 s from `voice_of`; the local voicer is unchanged.
  - `LineVoicer` keeps the job it is on (`_current`). When `close()`'s drain runs out, it writes the line in flight
    and everything queued behind it now, in the caller, in order, unvoiced (`voice: null`, the `voice` push
    `ready: false`), and logs how many. The worker writes its record and clears `_current` under the queue's lock,
    and drops its late record for a line `close()` already wrote, so no line is ever written twice.
- **Regression tests** (`tests/unit/test_server_voice.py`):
  - `test_a_stop_during_a_slow_generation_writes_what_is_left_unvoiced_in_order`: a blocked TTS, a write and a
    second line queued, `close(0.05)`; all three records in order without a voice, both lines reported failed, the
    late answer writes nothing, the queued line is never voiced;
  - `test_a_stop_after_the_line_is_written_writes_nothing_twice`;
  - `test_the_tts_timeout_reaches_the_sdk_client` (a fake SDK client, no network) and the timeout in
    `test_voice_of_builds_the_synthesizer_only_with_a_key_and_a_voice`.
- **Verification:** the new tests fail to import without the fix; ruff clean, 3294 passed.

### #2 — the whole words after the voice (7a37ebc)

- **Change:** `caption_for` returns her whole words with the emoji. `forward` sends them as the caption when they fit
  1024 characters; otherwise it sends the voice without a caption, then the words through `send_text`, which chunks
  them at 4096. This applies to a stored file and an old record alike. `docs/TELEGRAM_SETUP.md` says so.
- **Regression test** (`tests/unit/test_telegram_outbound.py`):
  - `test_a_long_reply_goes_as_the_voice_then_its_whole_words`: a 2400-character stored line, a short one and a long
    old record → voice + the whole text, voice with its caption, voice + the whole text;
  - `test_voice_caption_carries_emoji_and_the_whole_text` replaces the test that pinned the cut.
- **Verification:** both tests fail without the fix; ruff clean, **3295 passed**.

## Architecture impact

No contract changed. Two behaviours are worth knowing for the next phase:
- the server's outbox writer never loses a line on a stop: what the drain didn't finish is written unvoiced;
- in Telegram a voice message is never the only place her words are: past the caption cap they follow as text.
