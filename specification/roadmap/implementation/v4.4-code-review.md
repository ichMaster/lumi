## Findings

### 1. 🟡 LOW — State and Schedule never refresh while open

**Where:** `VIEW_KINDS.state` / `VIEW_KINDS.schedule` in [desktop/ui/app.js](../../../desktop/ui/app.js).

**Failure scenario:** the views read once per mount. A Schedule left open shows «now 23:56» and the next due times of
that moment; after a firing, the row's last and next stay as they were until the view is opened again. State keeps the
day it was opened on past midnight. The same as the Journal and Thoughts (v4.3 review #4).

**Proposed fix:** re-read on the `state` push (a turn or a scheduled act publishes one) while the view is shown, or on a
minute timer for Schedule. Backlog, with v4.3 #4.

### 2. 🟡 LOW — the tool list is this window's last successful reply only

**Where:** `turn()` and `toolsBlock()` in [desktop/ui/app.js](../../../desktop/ui/app.js).

**Failure scenario:**
- a reply that fails (a model error, a broken stream) leaves the previous reply's tools in the panel under «Tools ·
  last reply»;
- a reply to a Telegram line, the terminal or another window never reaches this list, because `tool_calls` rides on
  the turn outcome only and the `message` push doesn't carry it (as the issue set).

**Proposed fix:** clear `st.lastTools` when a turn starts; carry `tool_calls` on her `message` push if the owner wants
other clients' replies listed. Backlog.

### 3. 🟡 LOW — a naive stamp in `schedule.state` makes `/v1/schedule` answer 500

**Where:** `Scheduler.view` → `next_due` in [core/schedule.py](../../../core/schedule.py).

**Failure scenario:** every stamp the scheduler writes carries the clock's offset (`2026-11-18T20:31:04+02:00`). A stamp
edited by hand without one (`2026-11-18T20:31`) meets an aware `now` in `max(now, last_fired + …)`, raises
`TypeError`, and the whole route answers 500. The scheduler's own `due()` would raise the same way on its next tick,
so this is not new with v4.4; the view only makes it visible.

**Proposed fix:** read a naive stamp in `now`'s zone (in `last_fired_of`, for the scheduler and the view alike), with
a test. Cleanup.

### 4. 🟡 LOW — with a day cap of 0 the view still promises tomorrow

**Where:** `Scheduler.view` in [core/scheduler.py](../../../core/scheduler.py).

**Failure scenario:** `scheduler.day_cap: 0` means nothing ever fires. The view moves each next due past midnight once,
so it shows «Tomorrow 08:30» for a directive that will not fire tomorrow either. Prod and dev run with 2,400.

**Proposed fix:** a cap of 0 gives every entry `next_due: null`. Cleanup.

## What's solid

- **Owner-only and read-only:**
  - both routes take the owner's token; the room's gets 403;
  - the room's turn builds its own outcome and never carries `tool_calls`;
  - the view stamps nothing, counts nothing, rolls no day and writes no file;
  - both routes answer while the turn lock is held (tested), and `/v1/today` never computes the mood or calls a model.
- **The next due time is the scheduler's own rule:**
  - `next_due` evaluates `due()` minute by minute from the earliest possible moment;
  - the quiet-hours veto is the scheduler's (every kind but `at`); a reached cap moves the next due past midnight;
  - the real schedule's view takes about 1.5 ms;
  - the tests assert that `due()` holds at every computed time.
- **Concurrency with the brain thread:** the view copies `schedule.state` and the counts whole (`dict(…)`), so a fire
  stamping meanwhile can't tear them.
- **Byte-identical with the trace off:** the outcome has no `tool_calls` field (tested on the blocking and the
  streamed outcome). With it on the list is bounded (20 calls, 120 / 160 characters, one line) and an image is a
  marker.
- **The same data as the commands:**
  - the biorhythms equal `/biorhythm` value for value;
  - the cycle equals `menstrual_phase` day for day, for 21-, 28- and 35-day cycles;
  - a seeds row's lines are read by the same `load_nudges` a fire uses (on dev, 10 and 8).
- **The page:** the SVG is built node by node; her reading, the phase note and the topics are text (`lang="uk"`); late
  answers for an old mount are dropped; the portrait panel's closeness cell keeps its padding with the cycle row added.
