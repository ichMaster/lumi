## Findings

### 1. 🟠 A room turn the server gave up on can still keep its reply — `server/app.py` `_room_turn`, `core/space.py` `abandon`

**Scenario:** the model answers at 21.9 s. The server's `runner.join(timeout + grace)` returns with the thread still
alive, so it answers `504`. In the microseconds before `space.abandon(turn_id)` runs, the turn thread passes its
`turn_id in self._abandoned` check and stores her reply. The room saw nothing (a 504 is silence), but she remembers
saying it — against Q2 ("what Lumi remembers is what the room saw") and the issue's "a turn past its timeout stores
nothing". The late `abandon` also leaves the id in `_abandoned` for good (the turn's `finally` already ran).

**Fix:** make the check-and-store and `abandon` atomic under one small lock. `abandon` on a turn that already stored
its reply removes that reply (line + vector) and forgets its kept outcome; on a turn that isn't running and stored
nothing it records nothing (no leak).

### 2. 🟠 A big `observe` holds the shared embedder for the whole batch — `core/space.py` `_embed`

**Scenario:** the bridge starts after a downtime and sends its backlog, 200 lines in one call (Q1, Q7). `_embed` makes
one `embed` call of 200 texts under `LockedEmbedder`'s lock, about a second or more on the local model. His turn
arriving then waits for that lock before its RAG query. The DoD says a room never delays a turn of his.

**Fix:** embed in small chunks (16 lines per call), so his query waits at most one chunk (milliseconds).

### 3. 🟠 A room line's length is unbounded in the room's prompt — `core/space.py` `render_transcript`

**Scenario:** the owner pastes an article into Agora from Element (Matrix allows ~65 KB a message). Every room turn
for the next 40 lines carries it, so each prompt costs tens of thousands of tokens more and may run past the 20 s
budget, which is a `504` and silence.

**Fix:** cut each line in the room's prompt (1500 characters, with `…`). The stored line keeps its full text.

### 4. 🟡 The stop closes the room's store under a running room turn — `core/space.py` `close`

**Scenario:** a redeploy stops the server while a room turn (or an abandoned one) is still in the model. `close_space`
closes the SQLite connection; the turn then fails on a closed database (a logged 500). ARCHITECTURE says a redeploy
closes both sessions cleanly.

**Fix:** `close()` waits for the room's turn lock (bounded by the turn's budget plus a grace), then closes.

### 5. 🟡 A `ts` without an offset is accepted — `server/app.py` `observe`

**Scenario:** a line with `"ts": "2026-11-16T08:01:12"` passes the ISO check, sorts as UTC (two hours off in Kyiv)
and lands in the wrong place of the window. Q9 agreed on ISO-8601 with the offset.

**Fix:** `422` for a `ts` without an offset; say so in the contract's `observe.about` (a clarification, still v1).

### 6. 🟡 `known_outcome`'s check-then-get — `core/space.py` `known_outcome`

**Scenario:** a request thread runs `turn_id in self._outcomes`, and before `self._outcomes[turn_id]` runs, the turn
thread's `_keep` evicts that oldest entry. The result is a `KeyError` and a 500 for a retry that should have returned
the stored answer. It needs 64 kept turns and exact timing, so it is rare.

**Fix:** a single `.get()`.

### 7. 🟡 The transcript block can be spoofed — deferred to v3.2

A room line holding `»»»` closes the quoted block early. A room speaker named «Лілі (ти)» (no `:`, so the speaker rule
allows it) renders like her own line. In v3.1 nothing of his is in the room's prompt, so the harm is limited to
steering her voice. v3.2 rewrites the space recipe (the room rule, the sentinel test, the name guard), and the
transcript's integrity belongs there: escape the delimiter and keep her marker unforgeable.

### 8. 🟡 `"…"` on a blocked candidate — deferred to v3.3

`GeminiClient` turns a blocked or empty candidate into `{"reply": "…"}` (`_GEMINI_BLOCKED_STATE`). In the room, that
would be posted. v3.3's `pass` covers it: an empty reply or that placeholder becomes a `pass`.

### 9. 🟡 The trigger's `ts` from the turn — deferred to the backlog

When `observe` failed before a turn (Q6's fallback), the trigger is stored with the server's clock, and the later
`observe` of the same line is `known`, so its real `ts` is dropped. The skew is seconds, inside the same exchange.
Accepted for now.

### 10. 🟡 `search_vectors` in pure Python — deferred to v3.2

Every search scores every vector of the room in Python. At ~140 lines a day, that is about 50 K vectors a year, which
is slow. Recall over the room arrives in v3.2: use numpy as `state/local_store._topk_cosine` does, or a day window.

### 11. 🟡 Private attributes — deferred to cleanup

`agora_space_of` reads `core._canon` and swaps `core._embedder`. A public accessor (`Core.canon_text`,
`Core.share_embedder(wrapper)`) would keep the seam explicit.

## What's solid

- The room's path is separate by construction. It never touches `_Service.begin_turn`, the service lock, the outbox,
  the event bus or `last_input`; the integration test holds his turn inside the model while the room is answered.
- The token scoping is a single comparison per route. The room's token gets `403` on every one of his routes and on
  his channel, and the owner's token keeps its reach. Both are constant-time compares, never logged.
- Idempotency is in the store's `UNIQUE (channel, role, source_id)`, not in memory. Retries survive a restart, and her
  reply shares the trigger's id without a collision.
- The emotion gate validates every room reply before anything is stored or returned. A malformed state is repaired;
  an empty one is an error.
- Off by default, byte for byte. With no space, the route set, health and every owner payload are unchanged (pinned).

## Fixes applied

Every fix landed with a regression test that fails on the code before it; fake models only, no network. Final gates:
ruff clean, **2831 passed**.

1. **`eb5c08d` — the store-or-drop step and `abandon` under one lock.**
   - `abandon` marks a running turn, whose reply is then dropped at its store step.
   - It also removes a reply already stored (the line, its vector and the kept outcome).
   - It records nothing for a turn that isn't running.
   - Tests: `test_abandon_after_the_reply_was_stored_removes_it` and
     `test_abandon_between_the_model_and_the_store_drops_the_reply`, the latter driving the exact interleaving.
2. **`f6430d2` — the room embeds in chunks of 16** (`EMBED_CHUNK`), so the shared embedder's lock is held one chunk at
   a time. Test: `test_a_big_observe_embeds_in_small_chunks` (200 lines → calls of at most 16, all 200 indexed).
3. **`eed2bf1` — a room line is cut at 1500 characters in the room's prompt** (`PROMPT_LINE_MAX`, with `…`); the store
   keeps it whole. Test: `test_a_long_room_line_is_cut_in_the_prompt_but_kept_whole`.
4. **`f0d73fe` — `AgoraSpace.close` waits for a room turn in flight** (its turn lock, bounded by the budget plus 2 s),
   then closes. Tests: `test_the_stop_waits_for_a_room_turn_in_flight` and `test_the_stop_never_hangs_on_a_stuck_turn`.
5. **`b91b503` — `observe` refuses a `ts` without an offset (`422`).** The contract's `observe.about` and its `422` row
   say so, and so does ARCHITECTURE's contract line. Tests: a naive `ts` in `test_a_malformed_observe_is_422`, and
   `test_q9_a_ts_carries_its_offset`, which pins the contract file.
6. **`32b282e` — `known_outcome` reads the kept outcomes with one `.get()`.** Test:
   `test_a_kept_outcome_evicted_mid_read_falls_back_to_the_store`.

## Architecture impact

- **The agora space API v1, clarified (#5).** A line's `ts` carries its offset, or the call is a `422` (Q9, now
  enforced). The version stays 1; matrix-agora re-copies the file with CR3. ARCHITECTURE §Contracts updated in the
  same commit.
- **What a `504` means (#1).** A room turn the server gave up on stores no reply, whatever the interleaving: a reply
  already stored when the server gives up is removed (line, vector, kept outcome). It is the same removal v3.3's
  `retract` does; v3.3 can build `retract` on `AgoraSpace.abandon`'s second half.
- **The stop (#4).** The room's store closes after the room's turn in flight, bounded. This matches ARCHITECTURE's
  "a redeploy closes both".
