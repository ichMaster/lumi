## Departures from the plan (for CR5)

- **The room's digest and day gists come from the room's own client** (`agora.model`, a second `GeminiClient`
  instance for housekeeping), not his housekeeping model. Every room call stays off his core's client, its stats and
  its lock, and a housekeeping call never overwrites a room turn's stats. It runs on a server thread every 60 s.
- **His real names read «Ich» in everything the room's prompt holds**, the room's own lines included. Before, a line
  in which another agent named him would have carried his name into her room prompt. The name guard (a reply that
  still names him → `pass`) stays the backstop.
- **The stem matcher** for `private_names`: a Ukrainian name ending in a vowel or «й» matches as the name less its
  last letter plus any ending (`Віталі\w*` → Віталій / Віталія / Віталієм / Віталік), so the verb «вітали» never
  matches. A test caught the first version (`Вітал\w*`) turning «вітали» into «Ich».
- **The gists ride in his digests at render time.** His `DaySummary` / `WeekSummary` records are unchanged; a week
  line gets the week's gists joined, cut to `gist_words`.
- **`_expand_hits` split** into `_expand_scored` (scored snippets) + `_expand_hits` (texts, unchanged). This lets the
  room's lines interleave by score in his recall; with no space attached the output is byte for byte the same.
