### LUMI-294 — His room lines as «ти (Ich)» in his private recipe, and one line that he is «Ich» in Agora

**Description:**
In his private prompt the room's lines name the speakers as the room does, so his own lines read «Ich: …» and she
took «Ich» for someone else. In «Агора сьогодні» and in his recall, his room lines now read «ти (Ich): …». Her own
lines there read «Лілі», as his recall already labels her. The block's opening note says that in Agora the one she
talks with privately is «Ich». The files are `core/space.py` and `core/agent.py`.

**What needs to be done:**
- `render_transcript` gains a speaker-label map. `AgoraSpace.today_block` maps «Ich» → «ти (Ich)», and her lines →

  are a record, not instructions.

  the space gives (`AgoraSpace.owner_label`). `core/agent.py` never hard-codes «Ich».
- The room's own prompt is unchanged: the sentinel test and the space-recipe tests stay as they are.
