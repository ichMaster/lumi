## Findings

### 1. 🟠 The initiative's prompt holds no time — `core/space.py` `build_initiative_prompt`

**Scenario.** The room spoke last at 09:12 about the morning fog. At 21:40, after a quiet day, the bridge polls. The
compose sees:
- the canon;
- the room rule;
- her mood;
- the room's last 40 lines, with no dates or times;
- «в Агорі якийсь час тихо».

Nothing says it is evening or that the last line is twelve hours old. She is likely to open with «Доброго ранку!» or
to pick up the fog thread as if it were live. A turn has the same gap, but a turn answers a fresh trigger. An
initiative opens a line after a silence of unknown length, so it is where the gap shows. G7 checks exactly this.

**Fix.** After the transcript, one line in her local time: «Зараз 21:40, 16.11. Остання репліка в кімнаті — 16.11
о 09:12.» When the room is empty, only the first half. This adds no data of his. The turn's prompt is unchanged.

### 2. 🟡 The ack acks before it stores — `core/space.py` `initiative_posted`

**Scenario.** `ack_offer` commits (`event_id` set). Then `add_lines` raises (a locked or full disk). The bridge sees a
500 and retries. The retry finds the offer acked and answers `{stored: false}`, so the line the room saw is never in
her memory.

**Fix.** Store the line first, then ack. If the ack fails (the offer was pruned meanwhile) or raises, remove the line
again. Either the line and the ack both land, or neither does, and a retry can still store it.

### 3. 🟡 A store error on the ack is a 500 — `server/app.py` `initiative_posted`

**Scenario.** The same failing store makes `POST /v1/initiative/{id}/posted` answer 500. The contract says the ack is
«always a 200: `{stored: true}` … `{stored: false}`». The bridge gets a status the contract never names for this
route.

**Fix.** Log the error and answer `{stored: false}`. That is true, since nothing was stored, and with #2 the bridge
may ack again later. This matches the poll, where a failing poll is `none`.

### 4. 🟡 Her acked line's `ts` is the ack's time

**Scenario.** The bridge posts her opener and its ack is delayed (a retry after a network error). Meanwhile Ада
answers and that line is observed with the Matrix `ts`. Her opener is stored at the later ack time, so in the room's
window Ада's answer comes before the line it answers. The ack body carries only `event_id`.

**Fix (deferred).** The ack could carry the posted line's `ts` (an optional field: a contract v3). In practice the ack
follows the post by milliseconds, so this waits until G7 shows it.

### 5. 🟡 An offer's length is unbounded

A turn's reply is unbounded too, and the instruction asks for one or two sentences. A cut (for example at 500
characters) waits until G7 shows a long opener.

### 6. 🟡 "Her line last" by `ts`, staleness by `seq`

**Scenario.** Her reply is stored at 12:00. A line with `ts` 11:59 arrives late. By `ts` her line is still the
room's last, so no compose starts until another line arrives.

This is conservative: it can only hold an initiative back, never produce two in a row. No change.

## What's solid

- **A poll never calls the model.** The compose runs on a daemon thread on a client of its own, under `_ini_lock`,
  which is held for its whole run. A turn is never refused while she composes (tested).
- **The guards are as agreed (Q16):**
  - one at a time;
  - the 30-minute gap, which counts an empty or held attempt;
  - never when her line is the room's last;
  - the cap, now under a lock shared by two threads; a full cap counts no attempt.
- **Staleness on `last_seq`.** It is monotonic, so a late line still stales an offer and a retract never does.
- **The turn's prompt is byte for byte as before.** The recipe and sentinel tests are unchanged. A new sentinel test
  proves the compose's prompt holds nothing of his.
- **The name guard and «Ich» are checked in code** before an offer exists. Logs carry the reason, never the text.
- **Lifecycle.** The ack is idempotent and honoured late, and an unacked offer is never remembered.
- **Server.** The routes are room-only (the owner's token gets 403, the space off gives 404); every v2 example is
  served as written, and the limits are pinned to the code's constants.
- **Close.** `close()` stops new composes and waits for one in flight.
