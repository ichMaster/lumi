## Findings

### 1. 🟡 No pass counter — deferred to the backlog

A pass stores nothing, by design. Its rate on prod shows only as `agora` calls in `cache-log.jsonl` that have no reply
of hers in the room's store, and the log line «room reply passed: …» goes to `lumi.log`. If the owner wants to watch
the rate in v8.2, a per-call `outcome` on the cache-log row would make it visible. That is a small additive field,
left until asked.
