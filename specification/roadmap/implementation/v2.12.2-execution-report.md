## Detailed Results

### LUMI-270 — `Lili.app`

`scripts/make_mac_app.py` builds `~/Applications/Lili.app`:
- a launcher that runs the Mac prod client's window;
- her calm portrait in the macOS rounded tile as `AppIcon.icns`;
- a `launch.py` that names the process "Lili" and sets her icon, so macOS shows one running app.

The bundle holds no client code, so `prod_update.py` updates what it runs. The app was built and installed on the Mac.

### LUMI-271 — the face pick (#271)

- **`desktop/faces.py` `FacePicker`.** The v0.11 resolver chooses the folder (theme → emotion → calm → the default
  theme → the flat pack), and intensity chooses the file.
- **The proxy.** It answers `GET /face` → `{src}` (with the key) and serves `GET /faces/<png>` from the client's own
  pack. Neither route is ever forwarded; the contract pins `FORWARDED` unchanged.
- **Checked on the real pack:** quiet-collapse/sad at 0.1 / 0.5 / 0.9 → `02` / `01` / `03`.

### LUMI-272 — the portrait panel (#272)

- **The portrait:** two stacked layers with a 480 ms crossfade. It is driven by the connect state, every `turn` push
  (any client) and this window's own turns.
- **Around it:**
  - the caption from `captions`, never the emotion's name;
  - the theme with its scene;
  - the closeness name, "N of 5" and the meter;
  - the mood of the day, folded to two lines.
- **The empty state:** the portrait and caption only.
- **Below 760 px:** the top strip — a 52 px thumb that expands, the meta line, the mood row.

### LUMI-273 — the think block + the thoughts strip (#273)

- **The think block.** `think` frames stream into a block above her reply, in three modes (shown / collapsed /
  hidden). One message's block flips on a click; the mode is kept locally, with the first default from `think_show`.
- **The thoughts strip.** `thought` pushes gather in a strip under the mood, never in the chat. They are listed
  newest first with the unread ones counted; replayed thoughts are never unread.
- **The replay flag.** The server's `replay: true` flag now decides the messages' times and the "While offline" count.
