## Findings

### 1. 🟡 LOW — `/v1/thoughts` waits on the store's lock while a whole-store write runs

**Where:** [core/agent.py](../../../core/agent.py) `Core.thoughts_page` → `Repository.thoughts_for`;
[state/local_store.py:108](../../../state/local_store.py#L108) (`_guard_with_lock`).

**Failure scenario:** every public method of `JsonRepository` (and so of `SqliteRepository`, which prod runs) runs under
the store's one `RLock`. The thought page calls `thoughts_for`, so it waits while another thread holds that lock. The
longest holder is `_persist`, the rewrite of `store.json` after a thought, a summary or a fact. On prod that file is
several MB, and `LATENCY.md` measured the post-turn writes at seconds. A Thoughts page asked at that moment answers a
few seconds late. It never waits for the turn lock (the contract holds, and a turn that thinks for minutes doesn't
block it), and it never deadlocks: the lock is released after each method.

The v4.3 issues file said "reading it needs no lock"; that is true of the turn lock only.

**Proposed fix:** none now. The store's single lock is the v1.5 design, and the server database (the v9 storage work)
replaces it. Until then a few seconds' wait on a read view is acceptable.

### 2. 🟡 LOW — every journal listing reads every day in full

**Where:** [core/journal.py](../../../core/journal.py) `list_days`.

**Failure scenario:** the listing parses each day to count its sections and find its stamp, reading up to 256 KB of each
of up to 5,000 days. That is 143 files of at most 7 KB on prod today (about 1 MB a listing, milliseconds), and her tool
caps one write at 4,000 characters. At years of entries, each opening of the Journal reads the whole journal again.

**Proposed fix:** cache the per-day summary (sections, stamp) by path and `mtime_ns`, as the thumbnails are. Backlog.

### 3. 🟡 LOW — a day that starts with a UTF-8 BOM loses its stamp

**Where:** [core/journal.py](../../../core/journal.py) `parse_entry`.

**Failure scenario:** a day file saved by hand in an editor that writes a BOM starts with `﻿# 2026-11-16`. The
first line is no longer taken as the heading, so the stamp lines don't follow it, the stamp is `null`, and the heading
and the stamp lines show as an untimed first section. Her tool never writes a BOM, and no prod day has one (counted on
2026-11-18).

**Proposed fix:** drop a leading `﻿` before parsing, with a test. Cleanup.

### 4. 🟡 LOW — Journal and Thoughts never refresh while open

**Where:** `VIEW_KINDS.journal` / `VIEW_KINDS.thoughts` in [desktop/ui/app.js](../../../desktop/ui/app.js).

**Failure scenario:** a section she appends to today's journal (`%journal` fires at 23:50) or a thought she has while
the view is open appears only after the view is opened again. The strip beside the portrait shows the new thought at
once, from the push channel. The issues file put a live update out of v4.3 scope.

**Proposed fix:** prepend a pushed `thought` to the Thoughts view when it shows the newest page and the thought matches
the filters; for the Journal, a refresh button or a re-read on focus. Backlog.

### 5. 🟡 LOW — a picture inside a paragraph shows as `!alt`

**Where:** `readerInline` in [desktop/ui/app.js](../../../desktop/ui/app.js) (v4.1).

**Failure scenario:** the reader renders a picture only on a line of its own. In `text ![alt](x.png)` the link rule
matches `[alt](x.png)` and leaves the `!`, so the line reads `text !alt`. In the journal (links and pictures as text)
and in the Files reader alike. No prod journal day has an inline picture.

**Proposed fix:** an inline picture token in `READER_INLINE` that renders as the alt text in the journal and as a link to
the picture in the reader. Cleanup.

### 6. 🟡 LOW — a rare kind's header says "about 0 a day"

**Where:** `thoughtsSummary` in [desktop/ui/app.js](../../../desktop/ui/app.js).

**Failure scenario:** with the Kind filter on `gaze` (one thought on prod over 162 days) `per_day` is 0.006, which the
header rounds to "1 · about 0 a day · 0% spoken".

**Proposed fix:** below 0.1 a day, word it per week or per month, with a design string. Cleanup.

## What's solid

- **The journal is the owner's only:**
  - the room's token gets 403 from the token check;
  - a server for another user gets 403 from `may_read_journal` on both routes, before its folder is looked at, even
    with its own folder present;
  - `features.journal` is false there, so its window shows no tab.
- **The thoughts go through the surfacing filter:** `Core.thoughts_page` reads only `thoughts_for(user_id, "")`, never
  the global `thoughts_since`. A test pages the stream one by one and per kind with another user's thoughts mixed in;
  `seeds` and `user_id` never leave.
- **Read-only:** the journal folder and the store are byte-identical after every route; no route takes the turn lock or
  calls a model (a test holds the turn lock and reads all three).
- **The day parser opens every prod shape:**
  - the full stamp; the stamp without a forecast; the day written before the stamp with its own headings;
  - Windows line ends; a broken byte (U+FFFD);
  - a malformed or impossible date and Unicode digits refused before any path is built; links out of the root and FIFOs
    skipped.
- **The cursor is stable:** it names a thought by its `when` and its place among those sharing it, so thoughts written
  between two pages never shift or repeat the older ones (tested with a thought in the same minute).
- **The proxy:** one pattern matched whole, `/v1/journal/<YYYY-MM-DD>`; a suffix, a traversal or any other name under
  `/v1/journal/` stops at the proxy (tested live, none reached the server).
- **The page:**
  - every node is built and every text set with `textContent`;
  - her journal prose goes through the reader's renderer with links and pictures kept as text, since the journal lives
    outside her sandbox;
  - late answers for an old mount or old filters are dropped;
  - ← → step days only when the focus isn't in a field and no other handler took the key.
