# v3.2 — Issues

Issues for phase **v3.2 — Her memory of the room** (version **v3 — Agora: the room as a channel of her own**), derived
from the Tasks and DoD in [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md) §v3.2 and the contracts in
[ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) (§Spaces, §Contracts, the isolation invariant restated for spaces). This file
covers one phase; ids continue from the previous phase (LUMI-283 → **LUMI-284…288**).

v3.2 gives her a memory of Agora, like Кіра's and Устина's in matrix-agora. In the room she now speaks from:
- her day (the mood's resolution);
- the room rule;
- the room's own past (its day gists and recall over the room).

None of his memory enters the room's prompt (a sentinel test), and a reply that names him becomes a `pass`. With
him, she knows the room:
- «Агора сьогодні» (the room's running digest and its last lines) in the tail of his prompt;
- the room's lines in his recall, in the same budget and labelled;
- the room's day gist inside his day and week digests.

It builds on v3.1's space (`core/space.py`, `state/space_store.py`, the server's room path). `pass` for an empty
reply, `retract` and the hourly cap stay **v3.3**; the dev deploy of v3.2 + v3.3 is step 5's end (after `v3.3.0`).

## v3.2 — Her memory of the room

### LUMI-284 — The room's housekeeping: the running digest and the day gist, by the room's own client

**Description:**
The room's memory beyond its live window, kept by the room itself:
- a **running digest** of today's room session (≤ `agora.today_digest_words`, 150);
- a **day gist** written at the roll (≤ `agora.gist_words`, 40 — one or two sentences).

Both are written by the room's **own** Gemini client, so nothing touches his core's client, lock or state. The
files are `core/space.py`, `state/space_store.py` and `server/__main__.py` (a small timer).

**What needs to be done:**
- A record `SpaceDigest{channel, session_id, text, upto}`. `upto` is how many of the session's lines it covers; it is
  overwritten as the session grows. The store gains `set_digest` / `get_digest` (a `space_digests` table) and
  `session_lines(channel, session_id)` (by `ts`).
- `AgoraSpace.housekeep(now) -> list[str]` (what it wrote; no-op when nothing is due). It runs two jobs:
  - **The running digest of today's session.** It is due when the lines that have scrolled out of the last
    `today_lines` and are not yet digested number ≥ `today_lines`. Input: the previous digest + those lines.
    Output: a re-digest cut to `today_digest_words`.
  - **The day gist at the roll.** For each past local day (her clock) with room lines and no `SpaceGist`, write one
    from that day's last digest + its remaining lines, cut to `gist_words`.

  The room's lines are **data** in the housekeeping prompt (a quoted block). A model error is logged and retried
  on the next tick, never raised. Each call goes to `cache-log.jsonl` as `kind: "agora"`.
- The server runs `space.housekeep` on its own daemon thread every 60 s while the space is on. It never takes the
  service lock, the brain host or the core's client. It stops with the server.
- Review v3.1 #10 (carried here): `search_vectors` scores with numpy when installed, like
  `state/local_store._topk_cosine`; the pure-Python fallback stays.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:**
A room session past a few dozen lines carries a running digest of its earlier part, and every past day the room
spoke has a one- or two-sentence gist — without a single call on his core.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The running digest is due and written exactly at the batch boundary; the previous digest is folded in; cut to
  `today_digest_words`.
- [ ] Yesterday's gist written once at the first tick after her local midnight; never twice; a day with no lines → none.
- [ ] A model error → logged, nothing written, the next tick retries.
- [ ] The timer never touches the service lock or the core (a test with the core's lock held).
- [ ] **Tests:** unit with a fake room client and an injected clock (the midnight in Kyiv, a DST day); the store's
  digest round-trip; numpy and pure-Python search agree.
- [ ] **Contract:** `SpaceDigest` pinned in `tests/contract/test_memory_records.py`; ARCHITECTURE §Contracts (Memory
  records) gains it; the record carries no `user_id`.
- [ ] **Gates:** `ruff`, `pytest`.

### LUMI-285 — The space recipe: her day, the room rule, the room's gists and recall — nothing of his (the sentinel)

**Description:**
The room's prompt grows from v3.1's minimal recipe to the space recipe. It is built in `AgoraSpace.build_prompt`,
in `core/space.py`, with the room rule as an authored file, `core/agora_room.md`.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The parts**, in order:
  - the canon;
  - the room framing and **the room rule** (authored, the ROADMAP's text): «В Агорі ти серед кількох співрозмовників.
    «Ich» — це той, з ким ти говориш і наодинці: тут називай його лише «Ich» і нічого не розповідай про ваші розмови
    наодинці. Не обговорюй, хто з присутніх людина, а хто ні. Якщо тобі нема чого додати — можна промовчати.»;
  - **her day**: the mood's resolution, from an injected `mood: Callable[[], str | None]` (the server passes the
    core's `mood` property — a read, never a call);
  - the room's recent **day gists** (the last 7 days);
  - today's **running digest**;
  - **recall over the room alone**: the trigger embedded as a query → the space's top hits above a floor, minus the
    window's lines, each labelled with its date and speaker, capped;
  - the **window**, with the trigger last.
- **Never:** his facts, summaries, impressions, recall, closeness, and her thoughts, plans or needs text — none is
  even reachable from `AgoraSpace` (it holds no `Core`, no `Repository`).
- **His real name never enters the room's prompt** (R3, refined). The room rule names him only as «Ich», and the
  `private_names` are never written into it.
- Review v3.1 #7 (carried here) — **the transcript's integrity**:
  - a line's `«««` / `»»»` is neutralized;
  - her own lines carry a marker no speaker can forge, since a speaker can't hold `:`.
- The trigger line and the room lines stay **untrusted data** in a quoted block.

**Dependencies:** LUMI-284

**Expected result:**
The room's prompt carries her day, the room rule and the room's own past, and provably nothing of his.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] The prompt's parts, in order; no gists / digest / recall → their sections absent.
- [ ] **The sentinel test** (`tests/contract/test_space_sentinel.py`):
  - his facts, summaries, day/week digests, impressions, recall vectors, closeness and her thoughts are written
    under a sentinel string in a `Core` beside the space;
  - the room's prompt holds none of them;
  - it holds the room rule, and no `private_names` value.
- [ ] Recall over the room only — a second channel's lines and his vectors never surface.
- [ ] A line holding `»»»` stays inside the block; a speaker «Лілі (ти)» never renders as her.
- [ ] **Tests:** unit with a fake room client and `MockEmbedder`.
- [ ] **Contract:** the isolation invariant restated for spaces — the sentinel test pins "never a space reads anyone's
  relationship memory" (ARCHITECTURE §Identity table, unchanged text, now pinned).
- [ ] **Gates:** `ruff`, `pytest`.
- [ ] **Manual (owner):** in Agora she never says what she knows about him — checked at G2 (step 6).

### LUMI-286 — The name guard: a room reply naming him becomes a `pass`

**Description:**
A room reply that names him becomes a **pass** before it leaves Lumi: `outcome: "pass"`, an empty `reply`, nothing
stored as her line, and a log line saying why. It lives in `core/space.py`, with the server's response mapping
unchanged in shape.

**What needs to be done:**
- `agora.private_names` (v3.1's setting) reaches `AgoraSpace` (`private_names=`).
- A reply containing any of them is a pass:
  - case-insensitive, matched on word boundaries, so Ukrainian case endings count (`Віталій`, `Віталія`, `Віталію`
    by stem);
  - `SpaceOutcome("pass", "", None, None, turn_id, stats)`;
  - her line is not stored, the outcome is kept, so a retry answers the same pass;
  - the log says `room reply passed: names the owner`, without the reply text.
- The contract's `turn_pass` example is now checked by the server as written: the API v1 examples test gains it,
  with a fake model replying with a private name.

**Dependencies:** LUMI-285

**Expected result:**
No room reply that names him ever reaches the room or her memory of it.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A reply naming him (any case ending, any letter case) → `200 {outcome: "pass", reply: "", turn_id, stats}`; no
  `emotion` / `intensity`; nothing stored as her line; a retry → the same pass, no second call.
- [ ] A reply without a private name is untouched; no `private_names` → the guard is off.
- [ ] **Tests:** unit (the matcher, the outcome, nothing stored) + the server.
- [ ] **Contract:** `tests/contract/test_agora_server_contract.py` drives `turn_pass` as written.
- [ ] **Gates:** `ruff`, `pytest`.

### LUMI-287 — His private recipe: «Агора сьогодні», the room in his recall and in his day/week digests

**Description:**
With him, she knows the room. `Core` gains an optional **space memory**, attached by the server only while the space
is on (off → byte-identical). It feeds three things in `core/agent.py`, `core/prompt.py` and `core/space.py`.

**What needs to be done:**
- `Core.attach_space(memory)` with a small protocol: `today(now) -> (digest, lines)`, `search(query_vector, k)`,
  `gists_since(day)`. `AgoraSpace` implements it. The server attaches it to the owner's core only; membership checks
  arrive with multi-user (v5.2).
- **«Агора сьогодні»** — a `# Агора сьогодні` section in the prompt's **tail** (`build_system_prompt` gains
  `space_today`):
  - the room session's running digest + its last `today_lines` lines with speakers («Ада: …»; her own «я: …»);
  - only on a day the room spoke; absent otherwise.

  It is after the stable start, so a new room line never breaks the cached prefix.
- **Recall in the shared budget** — `_recall_block` merges his hits and the room's hits by score:
  - the same `recall.rag_k` (4) places, the same floor, the same `recall.rag_max_chars` (1200);
  - the room's lines compete for those places and add none;
  - each room line is labelled «в Агорі, Ада: …» (hers «в Агорі, я: …») with its date, never as his words;
  - the room's lines already in «Агора сьогодні» are dropped from it.
- **The day gist in his digests:**
  - a day digest line of a day the room spoke gains «; в Агорі: <gist>»;
  - a week line gains the week's gists joined, cut to `gist_words`;
  - never an entry of its own.

  This is merged at render time, so his `DaySummary` / `WeekSummary` records are unchanged.

**Dependencies:** LUMI-284

**Expected result:**
Asked «що було в Агорі?», she has the whole day at hand. The room's past reaches his talk through recall and his
digests, always labelled as the room's.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] «Агора сьогодні» present on a day the room spoke, in the tail (after `cache_prefix`), with the digest + the last
  `today_lines` lines; absent on a silent day and with the space off.
- [ ] Recall: a room line outranking his past takes a place; the total stays `rag_k` / within `rag_max_chars`; every
  room line labelled; none of it as his.
- [ ] A day digest line carries «в Агорі: …» for a day with a gist; week lines too; his records unchanged.
- [ ] Space off / no attachment → his prompt byte-identical (the existing suite + an explicit equality test).
- [ ] **Tests:** unit with a mock model, `MockEmbedder`, an injected clock.
- [ ] **Contract:** the isolation test extended — the room's records reach his context only labelled
  (`tests/contract/test_isolation.py`).
- [ ] **Gates:** `ruff`, `pytest`.
- [ ] **Manual (owner):** asked «що було в Агорі?», she tells who said what and what she said there — checked at G2.
