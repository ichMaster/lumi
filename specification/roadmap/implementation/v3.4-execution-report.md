### LUMI-297 — the poll and the compose (#297)

**Status:** completed · **Commit:** 817fb7e
- `AgoraSpace` takes three new arguments: `initiative`, `initiative_llm` and `initiative_rule`.
- **Constants:** `INITIATIVE_FRESH_S`, `INITIATIVE_COMPOSE_GAP_S` (1800) and `INITIATIVE_KEPT_S` (86400). They equal
  the contract's `limits`; there are no knobs.
- **`poll_initiative()`** never calls the model. It returns a fresh offer, or starts one background compose when the
  guards allow:
  - one compose at a time: `_ini_lock`, held for the compose's whole run;
  - the gap since the last attempt;
  - the room's last line is not hers;
  - the cap: `_take_a_call`, now under `_cap_lock`.
- **Fresh** means: not acked, younger than 30 min, and `last_seq` not newer than the offer's.
- **The compose's guards:** an empty answer, «…», a reply naming him or any «Ich» → no offer, logged as «initiative
  held: <reason>».
- **The recipe:** `_recipe` is shared by `build_prompt` and `build_initiative_prompt`. The turn's prompt is unchanged:
  the recipe and sentinel tests pass as they were.
- **The instruction:** `core/agora_initiative.md`, authored.
- **Close:** `close()` stops new composes and waits for one in flight.
- **Validation:** 22 tests in `tests/unit/test_agora_initiative.py` and 1 in `tests/contract/test_space_sentinel.py`
  (the compose's prompt holds nothing of his). ruff and pytest pass.
